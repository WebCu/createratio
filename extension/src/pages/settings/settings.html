      Time pauses when you leave a site. New times apply to new sessions and daily refills.

      How access works
      • On: requires an intention, a 60-second wait and explicit confirmation. Tracks creating/consuming time and supported actions for your creation score. The whole site blocks when time runs out, including editors and messages.
      • Off: unrestricted access with no wait, timer or new creation-score measurements. Keeps history and saved time; cancels the current budget, pending wait and daily refill. Re-enabling starts fresh unless above-goal access applies.
      • All tabs for a site share one active-time budget. Creating, consuming and neutral pages all use time while active.
      • Default follows your Default session time. Choosing another time creates a site override; choose Default again to remove it. Custom times support 1–240 whole minutes.
      • Time changes do not alter a session already running. At local midnight, an existing session refills once to its configured duration without accumulating. Never-opened sites stay blocked; ending a session cancels its refill.
