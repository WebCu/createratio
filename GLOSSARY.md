# CreateRatio

CreateRatio helps users create more than they consume through timed, tracked access on chosen sites. A separate browsing overview measures website time without affecting the creation score.

## Language

**Site integration**:
The website-specific rules that identify creating, consuming, neutral activity, and supported actions.

**Vetted site**:
A website whose site integration has been reviewed and accepted into CreateRatio's available catalog. Being vetted makes a site available, not necessarily active.

**Enabled site**:
A vetted site selected for creating/consuming measurements and, where supported, timed access. Users enable sites independently; newly added integrations are not enabled automatically.

**Limit & track**:
A per-site choice that combines timed access with creating/consuming measurements for the creation score. Off means unrestricted access and no new creation-score measurements.

**Creation tracking**:
Measurements of creating/consuming time and supported actions on enabled vetted sites for the creation score. On limited sites, these measurements require an active access session or optional above-goal access.
_Avoid_: Activity tracking, track activity

**Website time**:
Total focused browsing time across all websites, grouped by domain and independent of creation tracking. It does not classify creating/consuming, record actions, affect the creation score, or determine which site integrations are enabled.

**Recorded site**:
A website with recorded activity, whether its integration is currently available or enabled. Removing an integration does not remove that website's history.

**Clear history**:
The user's choice to delete recorded creation tracking and website time. Saved intentions, preferences, and access sessions remain separate.

**Access session**:
A temporary period of permission to visit a gated website after expressing an intention, waiting, and explicitly confirming access. Its shared active-time budget pauses away from the site and refills daily unless ended or disabled.

**Above-goal access**:
An optional exception to timed access for enabled sites while today's creation score is strictly above the daily target. It permits unrestricted access with creation tracking, without changing sites that are Off.

**Access limit**:
The maximum active-tab time available during an access session, shared by the site's tabs. It is not a consuming-time threshold or a creation-score target.
